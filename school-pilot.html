What you are evaluating

Will the AI replace the school teacher?

No. NOVA demonstrates AI-led explanations and lesson flow, but real student use requires human supervision, teacher overrides and approved curriculum. The pilot is for adult evaluators only.

Can real students sign in today?

Not with this evaluation edition. The profiles and reports are fictional. Production child accounts, verified permissions, school-specific access, durable records and safeguarding remain to be built and assessed.

Can I speak to NOVA naturally?

Yes, the demo includes a Realtime API voice path, but it requires a server-connected OpenAI project with billing. Browser microphone access needs explicit adult consent. Live connectivity and voice quality need real testing.

Can a school evaluate NOVA before purchasing?

Yes. The owner can share dedicated adult evaluation credentials for each school and walk decision-makers through the classroom, curriculum and dashboards. Credentials in this pilot are not real user accounts.
