# Selling NOVA: realistic first-customer playbook

## The customer to prioritize

Start with **school principals and school owners, not individual parents**. One B2B relationship can bring multiple classes and simplifies supervised rollout, lesson scheduling, training and billing. Parents should initially see approved progress reports through their child's school in a later compliant product, not buy unrestricted live child-to-AI sessions.

## What you can ethically sell now

- A paid **institutional discovery/design engagement** that includes a supervised adult demo and a written scoped proposal.
- A **conditional school pilot reservation** subject to the missing production features being built, verified and approved before any children use it.
- Approved curriculum consulting and teacher training you can genuinely deliver with existing tools.

Do not sell real child access, measurable progress analytics, 128 complete lessons, or hands-off unattended teaching as if they are already shipped.

## An effective school meeting

1. Show the **adult school tour** (`/school-pilot.html`) and explain what the school is evaluating.
2. Open **Live classroom** and demonstrate the imaginary-animal lesson. Use one question, one retry and one slide transition. Turn on live voice *only after you have verified it yourself*.
3. Show the Grade 1–8 curriculum outlines and explain the 16-week program. Be transparent that most lessons are not yet fully authored.
4. Show the teacher and parent reporting previews with their **fictional data** labels.
5. Ask: Which grades would benefit? Is there a smartboard? Does the school have stable Wi-Fi? Who would supervise? What is the approval process for student data and AI tools? What would make a pilot worthwhile?
6. Offer a written pilot scope, not unlimited access or unbounded API usage.

## Proposed commercial structure (illustrative, not verified pricing)

- **Setup/onboarding fee**: covers teacher orientation, curriculum mapping, school configuration and training.
- **Monthly school license**: covers approved class use, specified voice minutes or credits, support and updates.
- **Limits/overages**: cap AI voice minutes, generation requests, classes and concurrent sessions; obtain client approval for extras.
- **Pilot or annual contract**: with service scope, termination, support response expectations, renewal and payment terms.

Avoid an unlimited-voice offer until you have measured model usage and total cost. The owner console includes an editable contribution estimate, not a quote generator.

## How access should work in the REAL product

- The owner creates a verified school organization and accepts a paid plan.
- The school receives a unique secure administrator login.
- Admins add approved teachers and classrooms, with the system enforcing organization isolation.
- Students use age-appropriate accounts or school-controlled sessions only after all safeguarding requirements are met.
- Parents receive invitations tied to their authorized child's profile with verified relationships and permissions.
- Subscriptions are enforced on the server and all payments use a supported provider or proper manual invoice workflow.

**The current demo does none of this automatically.** It has password-protected *adult evaluation* access only.

## Suggested onboarding checklist for a future compliant school pilot

- Signed scope, terms, privacy and data processing documentation.
- Verified school administrator and data controller contacts.
- Grade and curriculum selections approved by educators.
- Child-safety review, verified provider eligibility and zero data retention where required.
- Guardian authorization and school approvals where legally required.
- Teacher supervision plan with easy pause/stop.
- Recording, retention, deletion and data export policy.
- Data security/tenant isolation and recovery tests.
- Actual AI voice usage caps and budget alerts.
- Test of a full lesson with adult testers before any school rollout.
- Teacher training and a support/incident escalation contact.

## What not to promise

Do not market NOVA as replacing teachers, identifying a child's hidden potential, diagnosing cognitive skills, guaranteeing learning improvements, or autonomously supervising minor children. The differentiator is structured learning with engaging AI support and human educational oversight.
